Gajga Plant

Plant Information

Botanical Name: Caesalpinia bonducella (L.) fleming

Common Name: Gajga

Family: Fabaceae

Description

It is a thorny scrambling shrub belonging to the legume family. The plant produces prickly pods containing hard, smooth, marble-like seeds that are typically greyish-green or yellow.

Medicinal Uses

  1. In traditional Ayurvedic medicine, the seeds, leaves and bark are utilized to treat ailments like fever, abdominal pain and swelling.
  2. Traditionally used to help manage fever.
  3. Used for abdominal pain.
